Peter had drawn near the fire in the courtyard and sat among the servants, holding his hands to the coals against the night chill. The light reached his face. Seeing Peter warming himself, one of the high priest's maids looked at him closely, and she knew that he had been with Jesus. She came across to him and spoke.

"You were also with the Nazarene, Jesus!"

Peter answered, "I neither know nor understand what you are saying," and he turned his face back toward the flames.

Presently he rose and went out on the porch, and the rooster crowed. He heard it clearly through the cold air.

The maid had followed him to the gateway, and she now spoke to those who stood by. "This is one of them."

Peter denied it again. "But he again denied it."

A little while later, those who stood by came to him and said, "You truly are one of them, for you are a Galilean, and your speech shows it."

Peter swore and cried out, "I don't know this man of whom you speak!"

At that moment the rooster crowed the second time. Peter heard it, and he knew the sound was the second.

Peter remembered the words that Jesus said to him, *Before the rooster crows twice, you will deny me three times.*
