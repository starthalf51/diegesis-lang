# 22권 — 구혼자들에게 밝히다 (22.1 ~ 22.7)

살해의 세부(22.8 이하)는 뺐다. 페넬로페는 이 자리에 없다 — 21권 끝에서 윗방으로 올라가 잠들었고(23.2 "waking me up
out of a sweet sleep") 에우리클레이아가 23.1 에 깨운다.

```sl
scene 22.1 at cloister
  when after 21.16
  enter odysseus, telemachus, eumaeus, philoetius, suitors, antinous, eurymachus
  odysseus says suitors: "The mighty contest is at an end. I will now see whether Apollo will vouchsafe it to me to hit another mark" src 22.1
  odysseus does shoot antinous "The arrow struck Antinous in the throat" src 22.2
  exit antinous
  # 22.3 "they thought that he had killed Antinous by mistake" — 아직 거지로 본다
  suitors says odysseus: "Stranger, you shall pay for shooting people in this way" src 22.2
  odysseus tells suitors with odysseus_home: beggar_is_odysseus "Dogs, did you think that I should not come back from Troy?" src 22.4
  eurymachus says odysseus: "If you are Ulysses, then what you have said is just." src 22.6
  odysseus says eurymachus: "Though you should give me all that you have in the world … I will not stay my hand till I have paid all of you in full." src 22.7
  exit all
```
