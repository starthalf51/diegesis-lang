# 23권 — 침대 시험 (23.1 ~ 23.19)

에우리클레이아가 두 번 말하고(23.1·23.3, 23.5·23.7 흉터), 텔레마코스가 꾸짖지만(23.10) 페넬로페는 그때마다 믿지
않는다(23.2 "you must be mad", 23.6 "what you tell me cannot be really true", 23.8, 23.11 "if he really is Ulysses").
그녀를 믿게 한 것은 오디세우스가 침대의 비밀을 말한 것이다(23.19 "Now, however, that you have convinced me by showing
that you know all about our bed … hard of belief though I have been I can mistrust no longer").

```sl
scene 23.1 at upper_room
  when after 22.1
  enter penelope, eurycleia
  eurycleia tells penelope: odysseus_home "Ulysses has at last indeed come home again, and has killed the suitors" src 23.1
  penelope says eurycleia: "My good nurse, you must be mad." src 23.2
  penelope doubts odysseus_home
  eurycleia tells penelope: beggar_is_odysseus "He was the stranger whom they all kept on treating so badly in the cloister. Telemachus knew all the time that he was come back" src 23.3
  penelope asks eurycleia: "if he has really come home as you say, how did he manage to overcome the wicked suitors single handed" src 23.4
  eurycleia tells penelope: odysseus_home "your husband is come home to find both wife and son alive and well" src 23.5
  penelope says eurycleia: "what you tell me cannot be really true. It is some god who is angry with the suitors … Ulysses is dead far away" src 23.6
  penelope doubts odysseus_home
  penelope doubts beggar_is_odysseus
  eurycleia tells penelope: beggar_is_odysseus "when I was washing him I perceived the scar which the wild boar gave him, and I wanted to tell you about it, but in his wisdom he would not let me" src 23.7
  penelope says eurycleia: "however wise you may be you can hardly fathom the counsels of the gods" src 23.8
  penelope doubts beggar_is_odysseus
  exit all

scene 23.9 at cloister
  when after 23.1
  enter odysseus, telemachus, penelope, eurycleia
  # 23.9 "she was misled by his shabby clothes and failed to recognise him"
  telemachus tells penelope: beggar_is_odysseus "Mother … why do you keep away from my father in this way?" src 23.10
  # 23.11 의 말 자체가 의심이다("if he really is Ulysses") — 의심을 그 말 앞에 둔다(말하는 순간 이미 믿지 않는다).
  penelope doubts beggar_is_odysseus
  penelope says telemachus: "if he really is Ulysses come back to his own home again, we shall get to understand one another better by and by, for there are tokens with which we two are alone acquainted" src 23.11
  odysseus says telemachus: "Let your mother put me to any proof she likes … She rejects me for the moment and believes me to be somebody else" src 23.12
  enter athena
  athena does glorify odysseus "Minerva made him look taller and stronger than before" src 23.16
  exit athena
  odysseus orders eurycleia: "But come, nurse, get a bed ready for me; I will sleep alone, for this woman has a heart as hard as iron." src 23.16
  # 23.18 "She said this to try him" — 시험이다. 침대는 옮길 수 없다(그녀는 그것을 안다).
  penelope orders eurycleia: "Euryclea, take his bed outside the bed chamber that he himself built." src 23.17
  beat bed_test:
    odysseus tells penelope with beggar_is_odysseus, odysseus_home: bed_secret "Who has been taking my bed from the place in which I left it? … There was a young olive growing within the precincts of the house … I made it the centre-post of my bed" src 23.18
    penelope does embrace odysseus "She flew weeping to his side, flung her arms about his neck, and kissed him." because beggar_is_odysseus src 23.19
    penelope tells odysseus: beggar_is_odysseus "Now, however, that you have convinced me by showing that you know all about our bed … I can mistrust no longer." src 23.19
  exit all
```
