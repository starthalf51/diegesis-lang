# 13권 — 이타카에 닿다, 아테나의 변장 (13.21 ~ 13.38)

```sl
scene 13.21 at phorcys_haven
  when after start
  enter odysseus, athena
  odysseus asks athena: "Tell me, then, and tell me truly, what land and country is this?" src 13.22
  athena tells odysseus: odysseus_home "so, sir, the name of Ithaca is known even as far as Troy" src 13.23
  # 13.24 "he did not speak the truth, and made up a lying story in the instinctive wiliness of his heart"
  odysseus lies athena: stranger_is_cretan "I heard of Ithaca when I was in Crete beyond the seas … am flying because I killed Orsilochus son of Idomeneus" src 13.25
  # 13.26 "He must be indeed a shifty lying fellow" — 아테나는 속지 않는다
  athena doubts stranger_is_cretan
  athena tells odysseus: "Did you not know Jove's daughter Minerva—me, who have been ever with you … tell no one, neither man nor woman, that you have come home again." src 13.26
  odysseus says athena: "tell me the truth, for I do not believe I am really back in Ithaca" src 13.27
  odysseus doubts odysseus_home
  athena does disperse_mist src 13.29
  odysseus sees: odysseus_home "the goddess dispersed the mist and the land appeared. Then Ulysses rejoiced at finding himself again in his own land" src 13.29
  athena tells odysseus: "I will begin by disguising you so that no human being shall know you … in the sight of the suitors, of your wife, and of the son whom you left behind you." src 13.34
  athena does disguise odysseus "Minerva touched him with her wand and covered him with wrinkles, took away all his yellow hair" src 13.37
  # 변장을 한 아테나와 변장된 오디세우스 자신은 "늙은 거지 = 오디세우스" 를 안다.
  athena believes beggar_is_odysseus
  odysseus sees: beggar_is_odysseus "she changed his clothes and threw an old rag of a wrap about him" src 13.37
  exit all
```
