# 16권 — 텔레마코스에게 정체를 밝히다 (16.1 ~ 16.44)

16.30-16.40(구혼자들의 회의, 페넬로페의 꾸짖음)은 뺐다. 에우마이오스는 16.15 에 성으로 떠나고 16.41 에 돌아온다 —
밝힘은 그가 없을 때 일어나고, 아테나는 그가 돌아오기 전에 오디세우스를 다시 늙은이로 바꾼다
(16.41 "for fear that the swineherd might recognise him and not keep the secret").

```sl
scene 16.1 at eumaeus_hut
  when after 14.1
  enter odysseus, eumaeus, telemachus
  odysseus says eumaeus: "Eumaeus, I hear footsteps" src 16.2
  eumaeus says telemachus: "So you are come, Telemachus, light of my eyes that you are." src 16.4
  telemachus orders eumaeus: "go, and give your message; then make haste back again … Tell my mother" src 16.14
  exit eumaeus
  # 16.15 아테나는 문간에 서서 오디세우스에게만 모습을 보인다: "Telemachus could not see her, and knew not that she
  # was there". 그래서 그녀의 말은 whispers(이름 붙인 상대만 듣는다)로 쓴다.
  enter athena
  athena whispers odysseus: "Ulysses, noble son of Laertes, it is now time for you to tell your son: do not keep him in the dark any longer" src 16.16
  athena does transform odysseus "she made him younger and of more imposing presence; she gave him back his colour" src 16.17
  exit athena
  telemachus asks odysseus: "Stranger, how suddenly you have changed from what you were a moment or two ago … Are you some one or other of the gods?" src 16.18
  odysseus tells telemachus: beggar_is_odysseus "I am no god, why should you take me for one? I am your father" src 16.19
  # 16.20 "But Telemachus could not yet believe that it was his father"
  telemachus says odysseus: "You are not my father, but some god is flattering me with vain hopes" src 16.21
  telemachus doubts beggar_is_odysseus
  odysseus tells telemachus: beggar_is_odysseus "Such as I am, it is I … At one moment she makes me like a beggar, and the next I am a young man with good clothes on my back" src 16.22
  telemachus does embrace odysseus "Telemachus threw his arms about his father and wept" because beggar_is_odysseus src 16.23
  odysseus orders telemachus: "let no one know that Ulysses is within the house—neither Laertes, nor yet the swineherd, nor any of the servants, nor even Penelope herself" src 16.28
  telemachus says odysseus: "Father, you will come to know me by and by, and when you do you will find that I can keep your counsel." src 16.29
  enter athena
  athena does disguise odysseus "Minerva therefore came up to Ulysses, turned him into an old man with a stroke of her wand, and clad him in his old clothes again" src 16.41
  exit athena
  enter eumaeus
  telemachus asks eumaeus: "So you have got back, Eumaeus. What is the news of the town?" src 16.42
  eumaeus says telemachus: "I did not think of asking about that … but I can say what I saw with my own eyes" src 16.43
  exit all
```
