# 14권 — 돼지치기의 오두막 (14.1 ~ 14.21)

에우마이오스는 거지를 맞아들이고, 거지(오디세우스)는 주인이 돌아온다고 맹세하지만 에우마이오스는 믿지 않는다.
거지는 자기가 크레타 사람이라는 거짓 이야기를 한다(14.12-18). 에우마이오스는 그 이야기 가운데 오디세우스에 관한
부분만 믿지 않는다고 말한다(14.19 "that part about Ulysses is not right").

```sl
scene 14.1 at eumaeus_hut
  when after 13.21
  enter odysseus, eumaeus
  eumaeus says odysseus: "Old man, the dogs were likely to have made short work of you … But come inside" src 14.2
  odysseus asks eumaeus: "My friend, who was this master of yours … I may be able to give you news of him, for I have travelled much." src 14.7
  eumaeus says odysseus: "the wolves and birds of prey have long since torn Ulysses to pieces … he is dead and gone" src 14.8
  odysseus tells eumaeus: odysseus_home "I will not merely say, but will swear, that he is coming … Ulysses will return in this self same year" src 14.9
  eumaeus says odysseus: "Old man, you will neither get paid for bringing good news, nor will Ulysses ever come home" src 14.10
  eumaeus doubts odysseus_home
  eumaeus asks odysseus: "tell me also, for I want to know, who you are and where you come from" src 14.10
  odysseus lies eumaeus: stranger_is_cretan "I am by birth a Cretan; my father was a well to do man … my father Castor son of Hylax" src 14.12
  odysseus tells eumaeus: odysseus_home "There it was that I heard news of Ulysses … the ship was by the water side, and the crew found, that should take him to his own country." src 14.17
  eumaeus says odysseus: "that part about Ulysses is not right; and you will never get me to believe it. Why should a man like you go about telling lies in this way?" src 14.19
  eumaeus doubts odysseus_home
  odysseus says eumaeus: "I see that you are of an unbelieving mind; I have given you my oath, and yet you will not credit me" src 14.21
  exit all
```
