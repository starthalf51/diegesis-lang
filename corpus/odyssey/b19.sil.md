# 19권 — 페넬로페와의 대화, 에우리클레이아가 흉터를 알아보다 (19.1 ~ 19.33)

페넬로페는 흉터 장면 내내 같은 자리에 있다. 그런데도 알지 못한다: 에우리클레이아가 그녀를 돌아보지만
"Penelope was unable to look in that direction and observe what was going on, for Minerva had diverted her
attention"(19.30). 그래서 에우리클레이아의 외침과 오디세우스의 입막음은 whispers(이름 붙인 상대만 듣는다)이고,
흉터를 본 것(sees)은 에우리클레이아 자신만 믿게 한다. 억제(waiver)는 쓰지 않는다.
에우리클레이아는 19.2-3 에 텔레마코스와 말한 뒤 여자들을 가두러 가고(19.5), 19.22 페넬로페가 부를 때 돌아온다 —
그 사이 텔레마코스가 거지를 "Father" 라고 부르는 것(19.5)을 듣지 않는다.

```sl
scene 19.1 at cloister
  when after 16.1
  enter odysseus, telemachus
  odysseus orders telemachus: "Telemachus, we must get the armour together and take it down inside." src 19.1
  enter eurycleia
  telemachus orders eurycleia: "Nurse, shut the women up in their room, while I take the armour that my father left behind him down into the store room." src 19.2
  eurycleia asks telemachus: "But who is to go with you and light you to the store-room?" src 19.3
  exit eurycleia
  # 19.5 "Minerva went before them with a gold lamp in her hand"
  enter athena
  telemachus says odysseus: beggar_is_odysseus "Father, my eyes behold a great marvel … Surely there is some god here" src 19.5
  odysseus orders telemachus: "Hush, hold your peace and ask no questions … Get you to your bed" src 19.6
  exit telemachus
  exit athena
  enter penelope
  penelope asks odysseus: "Stranger, I shall first ask you who and whence are you?" src 19.12
  odysseus lies penelope: stranger_is_cretan "There is a fair and fruitful island in mid-ocean called Crete … I, who am the younger, am called Aethon" src 19.15
  odysseus tells penelope: odysseus_home "I have lately heard of Ulysses as being alive and on his way home … Ulysses will return in this self same year" src 19.19
  penelope says odysseus: "May it be even so, but I know very well how it will be. Ulysses will not return" src 19.20
  penelope doubts odysseus_home
  enter eurycleia
  penelope orders eurycleia: "Euryclea, and wash your master's age-mate" src 19.22
  eurycleia says odysseus: "no one ever yet came who was so like Ulysses in figure, voice, and feet as you are" src 19.23
  odysseus says eurycleia: "Those who have seen us both have always said we were wonderfully like each other" src 19.24
  beat scar:
    # 19.30 "for Minerva had diverted her attention" — 아테나가 그 자리에서 페넬로페의 눈길을 돌린다
    enter athena
    athena does divert penelope "Penelope was unable to look in that direction and observe what was going on, for Minerva had diverted her attention" src 19.30
    exit athena
    eurycleia sees: beggar_is_odysseus "As soon as Euryclea had got the scarred limb in her hands and had well hold of it, she recognised it" src 19.29
    eurycleia whispers odysseus: beggar_is_odysseus "My dear child, I am sure you must be Ulysses himself, only I did not know you till I had actually touched and handled you." src 19.29
    # 19.30 한 대사가 사실 둘을 말한다: "I am at last come to my own home"(돌아왔다) + "to recognise me"(정체 시인) → 두 행위
    odysseus whispers eurycleia: odysseus_home "Nurse, do you wish to be the ruin of me … now that after twenty years of wandering I am at last come to my own home again?" src 19.30
    odysseus whispers eurycleia: beggar_is_odysseus "Since it has been borne in upon you by heaven to recognise me, hold your tongue" src 19.30
    eurycleia whispers odysseus: "I will hold my tongue like a stone or a piece of iron" src 19.31
    odysseus does hide_scar "Ulysses drew his seat nearer to the fire to warm himself, and hid the scar under his rags" src 19.33
  penelope says odysseus: "Stranger, I should like to speak with you briefly about another matter." src 19.34
  exit all
```
