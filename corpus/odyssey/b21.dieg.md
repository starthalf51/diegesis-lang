# 21권 — 활 시험 중 에우마이오스와 필로이티오스에게 밝히다 (21.16 ~ 21.22)

활을 당기는 시도들(21.1-15, 21.23 이하)은 뺐다. 밝힘은 성문 밖, 셋만 있는 곳에서다(21.16 "When they had got
outside the gates and the outer yard, Ulysses said to them quietly").

```sl
scene 21.16 at outer_yard
  when after 19.1
  enter odysseus, eumaeus, philoetius
  odysseus asks eumaeus, philoetius: "What manner of men would you be to stand by Ulysses, if some god should bring him back here all of a sudden?" src 21.17
  philoetius says odysseus: "If some god were but to bring Ulysses back, you should see with what might and main I would fight for him." src 21.18
  eumaeus says odysseus: "In like words Eumaeus prayed to all the gods that Ulysses might return" src 21.19
  odysseus tells eumaeus, philoetius with odysseus_home: beggar_is_odysseus "It is I, Ulysses, who am here. I have suffered much, but at last, in the twentieth year, I am come back to my own country." src 21.19
  odysseus shows eumaeus, philoetius: beggar_is_odysseus "See, here is the scar from the boar's tooth that ripped me when I was out hunting on Mt. Parnassus" src 21.19
  eumaeus sees: beggar_is_odysseus "he drew his rags aside from the great scar, and when they had examined it thoroughly" src 21.20
  philoetius sees: beggar_is_odysseus "when they had examined it thoroughly, they both of them wept about Ulysses" src 21.20
  odysseus orders eumaeus, philoetius: "Cease your weeping, lest some one should come outside and see us … do you, therefore, Eumaeus, place it in my hands" src 21.21
  exit all
```
