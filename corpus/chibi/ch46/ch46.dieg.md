# 46회 — 공명이 계책을 알아채다, 채씨 형제의 거짓 투항, 황개가 매를 맞다 (46.2 ~ 46.10, 46.68 ~ 46.127)

```sl
scene 46.2 at liang_boat
  when after 45.140
  enter lu_su, zhuge_liang
  # 46.6-8: 공명은 아무에게도 듣지 않고 계책을 알아챘다(45.143 주유의 짐작대로)
  zhuge_liang believes letter_forged
  zhuge_liang tells lu_su: letter_forged "The ruse succeeded well thus played off on Chiang Kan. Tsʻao has been taken in this once, but he will soon rise to it. Only he will not confess his mistake." src 46.8
  zhuge_liang says lu_su: "I know he is jealous and he only seeks some chance to do me harm." src 46.9
  exit all

scene 46.68 at cao_camp
  when after 45.129
  enter cao_cao, cai_zhong, cai_he
  # 46.68-71: 순욱의 계책을 받아 채모의 두 아우를 거짓 투항자(첩자)로 보낸다. 명령이 계획을 만든다(수행문)
  cao_cao decrees: cai_brothers_spies "he told them what he wished them to do. … But do not betray me" src 46.71
  cai_zhong says cao_cao: "Our families are in Chingchou and that place is yours. Should we dare betray? You need have no doubts, Sir." src 46.72
  exit all

scene 46.74 at zhou_camp
  when after 46.68
  when after 46.2
  enter zhou_yu, gan_ning, cai_zhong, cai_he
  cai_zhong lies zhou_yu: cai_brothers_defect "Our innocent brother has been put to death and we desire vengeance. So we have come to offer allegiance to you." src 46.74
  # 46.76 "They have come without their families and so I know their desertion is only pretence."
  zhou_yu doubts cai_brothers_defect
  zhou_yu believes cai_brothers_spies
  zhou_yu orders cai_zhong, cai_he: "he ordered them to join Kan Ning in leading the van" src 46.75
  exit cai_zhong, cai_he
  zhou_yu whispers gan_ning: cai_brothers_spies "They have come without their families and so I know their desertion is only pretence. They have been sent as spies and I am going to meet their ruse with one of my own." src 46.76
  exit gan_ning
  enter lu_su
  zhou_yu lies lu_su: cai_brothers_defect "But they wish to revenge the death of their brother. Where is the pretence?" src 46.78
  exit all

scene 46.82 at zhou_camp
  when after 46.74
  enter zhou_yu, huang_gai
  huang_gai says zhou_yu: "The enemy are more numerous than we and it is wrong to delay. Why not burn them out?" src 46.83
  zhou_yu says huang_gai: "But if you cannot show some injury you will not be believed" src 46.88
  huang_gai says zhou_yu: "Kill me; I do not mind" src 46.91
  # 46.86-91: 둘이 함께 매질과 거짓 투항의 계책을 짠다
  zhou_yu believes beating_is_ruse
  huang_gai believes beating_is_ruse
  exit all

scene 46.92 at zhou_camp
  when after 46.82 by 0d..1d                      # "Next day"
  enter zhou_yu, wu_officers, zhuge_liang, huang_gai, gan_ning, cai_zhong, cai_he, kan_ze, lu_su, lictors
  zhou_yu orders wu_officers: "Each leader is to prepare supplies for three months." src 46.92
  huang_gai says zhou_yu: "If you cannot, then it were better to take Chang Chao's advice, throw down your weapons, turn to the north and surrender." src 46.93
  zhou_yu orders lictors: "He ordered the lictors to remove Huang Kai and execute him without delay." src 46.95
  gan_ning asks zhou_yu: "He is a veteran officer of Wu; pray pardon him." src 46.97
  wu_officers asks zhou_yu: "Record his fault for the moment and after the enemy shall have been defeated then put him to death." src 46.99
  beat beating:
    lictors does beat huang_gai src 46.102
    # 46.108 "he will see to it that the two Tsʻao spies duly tell the tale"
    cai_zhong sees: huang_gai_beaten src 46.102
    cai_he sees: huang_gai_beaten src 46.102
    kan_ze sees: huang_gai_beaten src 46.123      # "Because I watched the General"
    lu_su sees: huang_gai_beaten src 46.104
    zhou_yu says huang_gai: "If you dare flout me again you shall have the other fifty." src 46.102
  exit all

scene 46.104 at liang_boat
  when after 46.92
  enter lu_su, zhuge_liang
  lu_su asks zhuge_liang: "You are a guest and not under his orders. Why did you stand by with your hands up your sleeves and say never a word?" src 46.104
  # 46.107-108: 공명은 이번에도 혼자 알아챘다
  zhuge_liang believes beating_is_ruse
  zhuge_liang tells lu_su: beating_is_ruse "Do you not know that that terrible beating was but a ruse? How could I try to dissuade him?" src 46.107
  exit all

scene 46.109 at zhou_camp
  when after 46.104
  enter lu_su, zhou_yu
  # 46.108 공명의 부탁대로 노숙은 공명도 성났다고 둘러댄다
  lu_su says zhou_yu: "He also resents it in his heart, and thinks you have made a mistake." src 46.113
  zhou_yu tells lu_su: beating_is_ruse "That beating that Huang Kai got is part of my ruse. I am sending him to Tsʻao Tsʻao as a deserter and so I have supplied a reason for desertion." src 46.116
  exit all

scene 46.118 at zhou_camp
  when after 46.92
  enter huang_gai, kan_ze
  kan_ze asks huang_gai: "Then this beating is just part of a ruse?" src 46.121
  huang_gai tells kan_ze: beating_is_ruse "How did you guess?" src 46.122
  huang_gai tells kan_ze: huang_gai_loyal "You see I have been very generously treated by the Sun family, all three of them, and have no means of showing my gratitude except by offering to help in this ruse." src 46.124
  kan_ze asks huang_gai: "I suppose you wish me to present your letter proposing to come over; is that it?" src 46.125
  exit all
```

공명은 장간의 일을 듣지 않고도 계책을 알아챈다. 조조는 채모의 두 아우를 거짓 투항자로 보내고, 주유는 그것을
알면서 받아 준다. 이튿날 황개는 모든 장수 앞에서 매를 맞고, 첩자인 채씨 형제가 그것을 본다. 감택이 서신을 맡는다.
