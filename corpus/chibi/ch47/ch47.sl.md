# 47회 — 감택이 거짓 항복 서신을 바치다 (47.2 ~ 47.65)

```sl
# 47.5 "The letter is already written": 황개는 오에 충성한다(46.89, 46.124). 그러니 이 서신은 글로 한 거짓말이다
event surrender_letter_written "Huang Kai writes the letter of surrender"
  when after 46.82
  when before 47.6
  huang_gai writes surrender_letter src 47.5

event cai_report "the two Tsʻais report the beating"
  when after 46.92
  when before 47.6.spies
  # 47.36 "That is from the two Tsʻais. They are reporting the punishment of my friend", 47.59 "we have already told Tsʻao"
  cai_zhong writes spy_report src 47.36

scene 47.6 at cao_camp
  when after 46.118
  when after surrender_letter_written
  enter cao_cao, kan_ze, lictors
  role:chancellor asks kan_ze: "You are an adviser of Wu; what then are you doing here?" src 47.9
  kan_ze tells cao_cao: huang_gai_beaten "Now he has been cruelly beaten, for no fault, before the face of all the officers in Chou Yü's camp." src 47.12
  kan_ze lies cao_cao: huang_gai_defects "He is grievously angry about this and wishes to desert to your side that he may be revenged." src 47.12
  beat letter:
    cao_cao reads surrender_letter src 47.14
    # 47.16 "Huang Kai is trying to play the personal injury trick on me, is he?": 읽자마자 의심한다
    cao_cao doubts huang_gai_defects
    cao_cao says kan_ze: "Huang Kai is trying to play the personal injury trick on me, is he? And you are in it as the intermediary to present the letter. How dare you come to sport with me?" src 47.16
    cao_cao orders lictors: "He ordered the lictors to thrust forth the messenger and take off his head." src 47.17
    cao_cao says kan_ze: "If the thing was real and you were sincere, why does not the letter name a time?" src 47.23
    kan_ze says cao_cao: "Do you not know that when one is going to desert one's master and become a renegade one cannot say exactly when the chance will occur?" src 47.30
  beat persuaded:
    cao_cao says kan_ze: "I did not see clearly; that is quite true. I offended you and I hope you will forget it." src 47.31
    kan_ze lies cao_cao: huang_gai_defects "The fact is that Huang Kai and I are both inclined to desert to you; we even yearn for it as a child desires its parents." src 47.32
    cao_cao says kan_ze: "If you two could render me so great a service, you shall certainly be richly rewarded." src 47.33
  beat spies:
    cao_cao reads spy_report src 47.35
    role:chancellor orders kan_ze: "I must ask you to return to settle the date with your friend: as soon as I know I will have a force waiting." because huang_gai_defects src 47.37
  exit all

scene 47.45 at gan_ning_camp
  when after 47.6
  enter kan_ze, gan_ning
  # 47.46 "Kan Ning smiled. Just then the two Tsʻais came, and host and guest exchanged glances": 감녕도 계책을 안다(46.76)
  enter cai_zhong, cai_he
  gan_ning says kan_ze: "The truth is the General is over confident and he reckons us as nobody. We count for nothing." src 47.46
  cai_zhong asks gan_ning: "We think you seem much inclined to go over to Tsʻao Tsʻao" src 47.51
  cai_he tells gan_ning, kan_ze: cai_brothers_spies "The truth is that we are only pretended deserters, and if you two gentlemen are of our way of thinking we can manage things for you." src 47.55
  gan_ning lies cai_zhong, cai_he: gan_ning_defects "Then this is the very heaven-given chance." src 47.58
  kan_ze lies cai_zhong, cai_he: huang_gai_defects "The fact is I have given the Minister a letter on behalf of Huang Kai and he sent me back again to settle the date of his desertion" src 47.60
  exit all

event letters_sent "letters to Tsʻao from the two Tsʻais and from Kʻan Tsê"
  when after 47.45
  when before 47.63
  cai_zhong writes cai_letter src 47.62
  kan_ze writes kan_letter src 47.62

scene 47.63 at cao_camp
  when after 47.45
  when after letters_sent
  enter cao_cao, cao_officers, jiang_gan
  cao_cao reads cai_letter src 47.63
  cao_cao reads kan_letter src 47.63
  # 47.63 "when Tsʻao Tsʻao got these two letters he was still doubtful … Only I still distrust the whole thing."
  cao_cao doubts huang_gai_defects
  cao_cao says cao_officers: gan_ning_defects "On the other side Kan Ning has been put to shame by the Commander-in-Chief whom he is prepared to betray for the sake of revenge." src 47.63
  cao_cao says cao_officers: huang_gai_beaten "Huang Kai has been punished and sent Kʻan Tsê to propose that he should come over to our side." src 47.63
  cao_cao asks cao_officers: "Only I still distrust the whole thing. Who will go over to the camp to find out the real truth?" src 47.63
  jiang_gan says cao_cao: "I failed in my mission the other day and am greatly mortified. I will risk my life again and, this time, I shall surely bring good news." src 47.64
  cao_cao does dispatch jiang_gan src 47.65
  exit all
```

감택은 어부로 꾸미고 강을 건너 황개의 서신을 바친다. 조조는 처음에 고육계라며 감택을 베려 하지만, 감택의 말에
설득되고 채씨 형제의 보고를 읽고 기뻐한다. 그러나 감녕과 감택의 두 번째 서신을 받고도 여전히 의심하여 장간을
다시 보낸다.
