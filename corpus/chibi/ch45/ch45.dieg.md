# 45회 — 장간이 계책에 걸리다 (45.77 ~ 45.143)

```sl
scene 45.77 at river
  when after start
  enter zhou_yu, wu_officers
  zhou_yu asks wu_officers: "He enquired whether any one knew the names of the admirals" src 45.77
  # 해석: 45.77-78 은 이름과 수전 경험만 말한다. commanders_loyal(조조에게 충실함)은 원문이 직접 말하지 않는다 —
  # 45.140-141 "delighted at the success of his ruse", "Those two were my only source of anxiety" 에서 주유가 그들을
  # 반역자가 아닌 조조의 진짜 도독으로 보았다고 읽은 것이다.
  wu_officers tells zhou_yu: commanders_loyal "they told him Tsʻai Mao and Chang Yün" src 45.77
  zhou_yu says wu_officers: commanders_loyal "They have lived in our country a long time, and are thoroughly experienced in naval warfare. I must find some means of removing them before I can effect anything." src 45.78
  exit all

scene 45.80 at cao_camp
  when after 45.77
  enter cao_cao, cao_officers, jiang_gan
  cao_cao asks cao_officers: "Now the enemy have spied out our camp. What can be done?" src 45.80
  jiang_gan says cao_cao: "When I was a youth Chou Yü and I were fellow students and pledged friends. My three-inch tongue is still good and I will go over and persuade him to surrender." src 45.81
  jiang_gan says cao_cao: "Rest contented, O Minister. If I only get on the other side of the river I shall succeed." src 45.84
  cao_cao does dispatch jiang_gan src 45.87
  exit all

# 해석: 원문은 서신을 누가 썼는지 말하지 않는다. 주유의 계책이므로(45.140 "the success of his ruse",
# 45.143 "my game", 46.8 "The ruse succeeded well thus played off on Chiang Kan") 주유가 꾸며 두었다고 읽는다.
# 주유는 두 사람이 조조의 충실한 수군 도독임을 안다(45.77-78) — 그러니 이 서신은 글로 한 거짓말이다(deception).
event forgery "Chou Yü prepares the forged letter"
  when after 45.77
  when before 45.88
  zhou_yu writes forged_letter src 45.115

scene 45.88 at zhou_camp
  when after 45.80
  enter zhou_yu, wu_officers, taishi_ci
  # 45.88 "he laughed as he said … 'A guest is coming.'", 45.95 "I feared you might be coming on his behalf to try to persuade me"
  zhou_yu believes jiang_gan_envoy
  zhou_yu says wu_officers: "A guest is coming." src 45.88
  beat arrival:
    enter jiang_gan
    jiang_gan says zhou_yu: "You have been well I hope since last we met" src 45.90
    zhou_yu says jiang_gan: jiang_gan_envoy "You have wandered far and suffered much in this task of emissary in the Tsʻao cause" src 45.91
    jiang_gan lies zhou_yu: not_envoy "I have not seen you for a very long time, and I came to visit you for the sake of old times. Why do you call me an emissary for the Tsʻao cause?" src 45.92
    # 45.93 "yet I can comprehend the thought behind the music": 주유는 핑계를 믿지 않는다
    zhou_yu doubts not_envoy
    # 45.92 "Why do you call me an emissary?" 에 대한 답: "the thought behind the music" = 네 진짜 목적(사신)을 안다
    zhou_yu says jiang_gan: jiang_gan_envoy "Though I am not so clever a musician as Shih Kuang, yet I can comprehend the thought behind the music" src 45.93
  beat feast:
    zhou_yu says wu_officers: "He is an old fellow student of mine and we are pledged friends." src 45.97
    zhou_yu orders taishi_ci: "You take this and wear it for the day as master of the feast. This day we meet only as friends and if any one shall begin a discussion of the questions at issue between Tsʻao Tsʻao and our country just slay him." src 45.98
    zhou_yu asks jiang_gan: "Do you not think my soldiers a fine lot of fellows?" src 45.103
    zhou_yu asks jiang_gan: "Is it possible to move such as I am?" src 45.110
    exit wu_officers, taishi_ci
  beat night:
    # 45.108 "Chou Yü pretended to be quite intoxicated", 45.115 "Putting on the appearance of irresponsible intoxication"
    zhou_yu does feign_drunkenness src 45.115
    jiang_gan reads forged_letter src 45.115
    jiang_gan thinks: commanders_traitors "Those two were connected with Wu in the beginning" src 45.116
    jiang_gan does steal forged_letter src 45.116
    zhou_yu lies jiang_gan: cao_head_soon "Friend, I am going to let you see Tsʻao's head in a day or two." src 45.117
    enter wu_officers
    wu_officers whispers zhou_yu: "A man has arrived from the north." src 45.122
    # 45.124 "Chou Yü crept out of the tent, while Kan listened with all his ears": 장간이 엿듣도록 꾸민 말이다(45.88 의 귓속말 지시)
    exit zhou_yu
    wu_officers lies jiang_gan: commanders_traitors "Chang and Tsʻai, the two commanders, have come." src 45.124
    exit wu_officers
    enter zhou_yu
    jiang_gan does flee src 45.126
    exit all

scene 45.129 at cao_camp
  when after 45.88
  enter cao_cao, jiang_gan
  jiang_gan says cao_cao: "Chou Yü is very clever and perfectly high-minded, nothing that I could say moved him in the least." src 45.130
  cao_cao says jiang_gan: "Your failure makes me look ridiculous" src 45.131
  beat letter:
    jiang_gan tells cao_cao: commanders_traitors "Well, if I did not win over Chou Yü, I found out something for you." src 45.133
    enter cai_mao, zhang_yun
    cao_cao orders cai_mao, zhang_yun: "I want you two to attack." src 45.133
    cai_mao says cao_cao: "But the men are not yet sufficiently trained." src 45.134
    cao_cao says cai_mao, zhang_yun: "The men will be well enough trained when you have sent my head to Chou Yü, eh?" src 45.135
  beat execution:
    role:chancellor does execute cai_mao, zhang_yun because commanders_traitors src 45.136
    exit cai_mao, zhang_yun                         # 45.136 "Tsʻao bade the executioners lead them away to instant death"
    dies cai_mao
    dies zhang_yun
  beat realization:
    # 45.137 "it dawned upon him that he had been tricked"
    cao_cao believes letter_forged
    cao_cao doubts commanders_traitors
    cao_cao thinks: "it dawned upon him that he had been tricked" src 45.137
    enter cao_officers
    # 45.139 "Though Tsʻao knew he had been victimised he would not acknowledge it."
    cao_cao lies cao_officers: commanders_remiss "the two men had been remiss and so had been put to death" src 45.139
  exit all

event spy_news "Wu's spies learn of the execution"
  when after 45.129.execution
  when before 45.140
  wu_spies sees: commanders_executed src 45.140

scene 45.140 at zhou_camp
  when after 45.129
  enter zhou_yu, lu_su, wu_spies
  wu_spies tells zhou_yu: commanders_executed "Spies took the news to Chou Yü" src 45.140
  zhou_yu says lu_su: commanders_executed "Those two were my only source of anxiety. Now they are gone, I am quite happy." src 45.141
  zhou_yu orders lu_su: "I do not think any of them saw my game except Chuko Liang. You go and sound him. See if he knew." src 45.143
  exit all
```

장간은 옛 친구로 왔다고 둘러대지만 주유는 처음부터 조조의 세객임을 안다. 주유는 취한 척하며 꾸며 둔 채모·장윤의
서신을 장간이 보게 하고, 장간은 서신을 훔쳐 조조에게 돌아간다. 조조는 두 도독을 베고 나서야 속은 것을 깨닫지만
내색하지 않는다.
